About Me

I am a Master’s student in Artificial Intelligence at Università della Svizzera italiana. My work concerns how training objectives shape model representations, with a focus on preference-based training of language models and controlled analysis of learned latents.

I also work on GPU systems and parallel computing. In 2026 I completed the CSCS–USI Summer School on Effective High-Performance Computing and Data Analytics at the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS), Switzerland, with instruction from industrial practitioners including experts from NVIDIA. Practical experience includes CUDA C++, multi-GPU workflows, GPU-accelerated Python, and MPI with CUDA.

Research interests

  • — Large language models — preference optimization, controllable generation, low-resource settings
  • — Representation learning — graph neural networks, knowledge graph embeddings, latent analysis
  • — High-performance computing — CUDA, multi-GPU systems, parallel scientific computing
