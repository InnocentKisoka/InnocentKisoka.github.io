WHO I AM

AI researcher at heart.

I'm an MSc Artificial Intelligence student at Università della Svizzera italiana (USI) in Lugano, with a strong research focus on Large Language Models, preference optimization, controllability of latent representations, and generative AI for low-resource languages.

My current work centers on extending the Creative Preference Optimization (CRPO) framework to Swahili, aiming to improve alignment, generalization, and creative capabilities of LLMs in underrepresented languages. I have a deep technical foundation in attention mechanisms, representation learning, Graph Neural Networks, and scalable experimentation.

I'm eager to contribute to cutting-edge research in LLM alignment, agentic models, and multimodal intelligence. Previously, I completed my Bachelor's in Computer Engineering in South Korea on a full scholarship.

RESEARCH FOCUS

Research Interests

Large Language Models & Low-Resource NLP

Developing methods to improve alignment, generalization, and creative capabilities of LLMs in underrepresented languages, with a current focus on extending preference optimization frameworks to Swahili.

Preference Optimization & LLM Alignment

Investigating controllable generation through preference optimization (CRPO and extensions). Interested in making alignment techniques more robust and generalizable across languages and domains.

Representation Learning & Interpretability

Understanding how latent representations form in deep models, particularly in Graph Neural Networks and attention-based architectures. Work includes ICML 2024 replications studying topological overfitting and implicit bias.

Knowledge Graphs & Scientific Machine Learning

Reproducing and extending state-of-the-art Knowledge Graph Embedding methods (GoldE, ICML 2024) and building Physics-Informed Neural Networks for complex physical systems such as cardiac wave propagation.
